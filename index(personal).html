About
+ Add headshot.jpg to your repo and set src="headshot.jpg"

Shaping the places people call home.

I'm an incoming Master's student in Urban and Regional Planning at UC Irvine (Fall 2026), and a recent graduate of Colgate University where I studied Environmental Studies and Geography — graduating magna cum laude.

My work sits at the intersection of housing equity, transportation access, and spatial analysis. I've led GIS research on refugee transit access in Utica, co-authored a study on post-COVID BART ridership, and completed senior capstone work on exclusionary zoning reform, drawing on abolition geography frameworks.

Outside planning, I'm a Division I sprinter, a radio show host, and a nail technician — all of which have taught me more about community than any textbook.

Transit Research

Post-COVID BART Ridership Analysis

Winter 2025–Present · Colgate Geography Dept.
+ Add project image

Collaborating with Professor Sun on a journal-targeted study analyzing post-COVID BART ridership using GIS, Python, and diverse datasets including BART trip data, ACS, QCEW, mobile location data, and nighttime lights. 03
Housing Policy

Exclusionary Zoning & Densification

Spring 2026 · Senior Capstone
+ Add project image

Analyzed single-family zoning (R1) as a mechanism of racial and class segregation, drawing on Ruth Wilson Gilmore's abolition geography framework. Traced historical links between R1, redlining, and the criminalization of homelessness, and evaluated zoning reform case studies in Minneapolis and Houston to argue that R1 abolition is a structural intervention toward more equitable housing.

Zoning Reform Housing Equity Policy Analysis

Climate Engagement

Chenango County Climate Education Plan

Spring 2026 · Environmental Studies Capstone
+ Add project image

Developed a climate change education and engagement plan for Chenango County EMC to support their Climate Smart Communities certification. Conducted stakeholder interviews and surveys, reviewed existing outreach efforts, and identified underserved audiences. Delivered an extensive case study review of successful climate engagement models.
